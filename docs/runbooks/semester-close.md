# Semester close

1. Preview the effect of closing the active semester.
2. Resolve pending session and invitation exceptions.
3. Close the semester, making cohort records read-only for non-admin users.
4. Verify active memberships transitioned to `alumni` and history remains visible.
5. Create the next semester from an approved template version.
6. Selectively carry forward returning mentors, startups, and administrators.
7. Review every carry-forward match before invitations are sent.
8. Archive the old semester only after operational reporting is complete.

Closing and carry-forward are separate auditable commands. Neither operation deletes identities, profiles, or historical participation.
