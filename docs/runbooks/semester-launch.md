# Semester launch

1. Create the semester in `draft` from an approved configuration-template version.
2. Confirm dates, invitation expiry, onboarding requirements, and default availability windows.
3. Upload the minimal roster and review every validation warning.
4. Resolve duplicate emails, returning identities, missing startup assignments, and role conflicts.
5. Commit the roster, then send invitations in a tracked batch.
6. Review delivered, failed, expired, accepted, and revoked invitation counts.
7. Process legitimate access requests.
8. Monitor the readiness dashboard and follow up only on required tasks.
9. Activate the semester after the launch checklist passes.

Never identify a send as successful until the email provider returns an accepted delivery response. Generated authentication links are credentials and must not appear in logs.
