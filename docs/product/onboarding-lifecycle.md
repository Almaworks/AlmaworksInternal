# Onboarding and offboarding lifecycle

## Product principles

- Ask only for the information needed to become useful.
- Supply sensible, versioned defaults and let users edit them later.
- Make progress visible and resumable.
- Never silently claim an invitation was delivered.
- Preserve a reusable account and semester history.

## Administrator journey

1. Create a Fall or Spring semester from a versioned template.
2. Import or enter the minimal roster: name, email, role, and startup assignment when applicable.
3. Resolve duplicates and returning-user matches in preview.
4. Send tracked invitation batches and review delivery outcomes.
5. Monitor onboarding readiness, resend or revoke invitations, and handle access requests.
6. Activate the cohort and manage exceptions.
7. Close the semester, producing alumni access and a carry-forward preview.

## Startup journey

An invited teammate authenticates by a verified email link, confirms identity, joins or confirms the startup, and completes essentials: company snapshot, team contact details, and shared availability. All active startup teammates may maintain company and team data. A checklist keeps optional enrichment visible after first entry.

## Mentor journey

An invited mentor authenticates by a verified email link, confirms identity, reviews an existing profile when matched, and completes essentials: short biography, expertise, and availability. Optional links and detailed preferences remain in the resumable checklist.

## Access-request fallback

People without an invitation may submit a request for the active semester. A request creates no membership and grants no data access. An administrator approves it into a tracked invitation or rejects it with an auditable reason.

## Essentials-first checklist

The initial wizard has three short steps: identity, role-specific essentials, and availability. The dashboard then presents a configurable checklist. Required tasks gate readiness; optional tasks improve matching but do not block entry.

## Offboarding

Closing a semester prevents new operational actions, preserves historical records, and changes cohort memberships to read-only alumni. Admins may selectively carry users and startup participation into the next semester; defaults are cloned from a named template version, never implicitly copied from mutable production state.
